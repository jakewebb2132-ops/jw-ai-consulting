# Navigating AI Knowledge Acquisition and Content Strategy

## Core insight
Effective AI consulting requires moving beyond surface-level trends to synthesize actionable, high-signal knowledge. By curating specific inputs, we build a more robust mental model that translates directly into client-facing GTM strategies.

## Key points
* Prioritize high-signal sources that provide structural insights rather than fleeting news.
* Use content as a mechanism to refine and stress-test internal knowledge systems.
* Focus on the intersection of technical capability and market-ready application.
* Maintain a consistent feedback loop between consumption and output generation.

## Why this matters for Jake
This approach ensures my consulting practice remains grounded in verified, high-leverage insights, directly feeding into the 'brain system' used for strategic client advisory.
