# The Flow Framework®
**Source**: [[2026-10-25-planview-spm-pom-research]]
**Last updated**: 2026-10-25

## What it is
A measurement system for value streams, created by Dr. Mik Kersten, CTO of [[planview]].
It is the measurement layer under the delivery stages of [[product-operating-model]].
SAFe 6.0 adopted its five metrics as the first five of its six flow metrics.

## Four flow items
All value-creating work in a value stream is one of four types:

| Flow item | Represents |
|---|---|
| **Features** | Business value |
| **Defects** | Quality |
| **Risk** | Security and compliance |
| **Debt** | Obstacles to future delivery |

The mix across these four is an **investment decision**, not a backlog accident. A value
stream running 95% features is borrowing against its own future capacity.

## Five flow metrics

| Metric | Question it answers | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Flow Velocity®** | How fast is value being delivered? | Flow items completed over a period |
| **Flow Efficiency®** | Is upstream work holding up delivery? | Active days ÷ (active + wait days) |
| **Flow Time** | Are we reducing time to market? | Elapsed time from entering the value stream to in the customer's hands, including waits |
| **Flow Load®** | Are we balancing demand and capacity? | Flow items in progress, active or waiting |
| **Flow Distribution®** | Are we investing in both value generation and business protection? | The mix across the four flow items |

Flow Efficiency is the one that usually shocks executives: most of a flow item's life is
spent waiting, not being worked.

## Why it matters in a customer conversation
It separates two things that get conflated. Velocity and deploy frequency measure whether
the factory works. Flow distribution measures what the factory chose to build. Neither is
outcome evidence. Adoption, business value, cost and risk are the evidence that closes the
loop back to [[strategic-portfolio-management]].

## Adoption reality
**80%** of organizations do not require daily inspection of flow metrics, per the Planview
2024 Project to Product State of the Industry. The framework is widely cited and thinly
practiced, which makes it a useful diagnostic wedge rather than a safe assumption.
