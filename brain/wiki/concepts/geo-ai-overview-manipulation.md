# GEO: AI Overview Manipulation via Press Releases
**Source**: [[2026-05-06-linkedin-articles-inspiration]] ("How $5 Press Releases Hack Google AI Overviews", Edward Sturm / Harperit)
**Last updated**: 2026-05-06

## Core insight

Google AI Overviews act like a parrot for low-competition brand queries. A $5–$20 press release distributed to established syndication sites can make Google's AI confidently describe your brand in your own words.

## Why it works

- Brand names are "under-targeted" — near-zero existing search results
- For non-YMYL queries with little coverage, Google AI fills gaps with whatever sources exist
- Distribution sites (barchart.com, etc.) have pre-built domain authority — no link-building required
- Unlike clone-site strategies, this requires zero technical infrastructure

## The playbook

1. Write a positive press release for your brand
2. Distribute via cheap service ($5 SEO Clerk, $15 Fiverr, or PR Log for better quality)
3. Wait ~1 week
4. Google the brand name — AI Overview should cite your language

**Do not** include direct backlinks to your domain — distribution sites may be low-quality and you don't want those links.

## Proof of concept

Harperit created a fake agency with zero DA, bought a $5 press release, and Google's AI Overview called it a "reputable SEO agency" with a "near five-star rating."

## Applicability

Useful for JW AI Consulting's brand presence and for advising startup clients on quick brand narrative control before organic search authority builds.
