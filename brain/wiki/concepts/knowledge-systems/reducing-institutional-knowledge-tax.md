# Reducing Institutional Knowledge Tax Through Documentation
**Source**: [[2026-05-22-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2053143980616138889?s=46&t=hKsCOqDSqhQTmxGk_H5iPQ)
**Last updated**: 2026-05-22

## Core insight
Product teams bleed productivity by constantly re-litigating past decisions due to fragmented information. By centralizing decision logs and customer insights into a searchable repo, teams can eliminate the 'context tax' that kills velocity.

## Key points
* Every fifth hour on a product team is lost to rediscovering previously made decisions.
* Context switching costs are high: 10 daily questions at 10 minutes each equals over 8 hours of lost time per person weekly.
* A shared repository for call summaries, decision logs, and analytics queries acts as a force multiplier for engineering autonomy.
* Documentation allows team members to self-serve historical context, preventing bottlenecks on senior leadership.

## Why this matters for Jake
This is a foundational element of building a 'second brain' for organizations; as a GTM strategist, I help clients implement these systems to ensure their internal knowledge scales as fast as their market presence.
