# Visualizing Dynamic Curriculum Knowledge Graphs for Education
**Source**: [[2026-07-12-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/lionel_mora/status/2074488408425066842?s=20)
**Last updated**: 2026-07-12

## Core insight
Marble App demonstrates how to transform linear educational content into a non-linear, interconnected knowledge graph. By mapping 1,144 concepts across 1,948 connections, they provide a structural view of learning that moves beyond traditional syllabus-based instruction.

## Key points
- Maps 1,144 distinct concepts across Math, Science, English, and History.
- Visualizes 1,948 relational connections to show how subjects interlink.
- Shifts from static curricula to dynamic, graph-based learning paths.
- Demonstrates the power of structured data in personalizing educational experiences.

## Why this matters for Jake
This serves as a prime example of 'knowledge architecture'—the ability to map complex domains into actionable, navigable systems, which is critical for building high-leverage GTM content engines.
