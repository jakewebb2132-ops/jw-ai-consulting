# Handling Unavailable External Content and Link Rot
**Source**: [[2026-07-08-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/milesdeutscher/status/2073172736617422916?s=46&t=hKsCOqDSqhQTmxGk_H5iPQ)
**Last updated**: 2026-07-08

## Core insight
When external resources like social media posts become inaccessible, it highlights the fragility of relying on live URLs for knowledge management. We must prioritize capturing and archiving content locally to ensure our internal knowledge base remains resilient against link rot.

## Key points
* Live web dependencies create single points of failure in research workflows.
* Automated scraping tools must include robust error handling for timeouts and platform-specific blocks.
* Shift from 'referencing' external URLs to 'ingesting' content into a structured, searchable format.
* Implement a fallback mechanism to capture screenshots or text-only snapshots when live scraping fails.

## Why this matters for Jake
As an AI consultant, my knowledge systems must be immutable and reliable; relying on broken links undermines the authority and utility of my internal brain.
