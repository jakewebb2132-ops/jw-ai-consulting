# Optimizing AI Agentic Workflows and Architecture
**Source**: [[2026-05-22-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/rohonchain/status/2052043443766194272?s=46&t=hKsCOqDSqhQTmxGk_H5iPQ)
**Last updated**: 2026-05-22

## Core insight
Agentic workflows are moving beyond simple prompt-response loops into complex, multi-step reasoning chains. Success requires rigorous architectural design to ensure reliability and output quality in production environments.

## Key points
- Shift from monolithic prompts to modular, task-specific agentic sub-routines.
- Implement robust error handling and state management for long-running processes.
- Prioritize deterministic control structures over purely probabilistic model outputs.
- Focus on feedback loops that allow agents to self-correct during execution.

## Why this matters for Jake
As a GTM strategist, building reliable agentic systems is the foundation for scaling high-value client operations without increasing headcount.
