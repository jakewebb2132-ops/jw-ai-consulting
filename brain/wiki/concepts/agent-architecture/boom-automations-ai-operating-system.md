## Core insight
Building high-value AI operating systems requires strict workflow mapping and process consolidation before touching any tools or writing code. Luke Pierce's framework proves that structured entity management and phased agent deployment deliver massive ROI for mid-market companies.

## Key points
- Map every workflow and time manual hours before selecting tools.
- Sort your software stack into three clear buckets: absorb, keep, or kill.
- Enforce exactly one write path per entity, documented in writing, prior to building.
- Consolidate one department at a time and avoid premature automation.
- Deploy four specific agents in a precise order, with intake always going first.
- Route simple tasks to cheap models and reserve frontier models exclusively for reasoning to cut token spend by over 80%.
