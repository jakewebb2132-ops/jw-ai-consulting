Infrastructure is the silent bottleneck of agentic workflows. Zongheng Yang highlights that scaling AI isn't just about model performance, but about the underlying systems that manage latency, memory, and throughput at scale.

* Memory management and KV cache optimization are critical for long-context agentic reasoning.
* Infrastructure decisions today dictate the ceiling for future product capabilities.
* Real-world production agents require robust orchestration beyond simple API calls.
