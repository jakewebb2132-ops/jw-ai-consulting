# Building Self-Improving AI Native Organizations
**Source**: [[2026-07-19-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/linasbeliunas/status/2077280821795311811?s=20)
**Last updated**: 2026-07-19

## Core insight
Building an AI-native company requires moving beyond simple automation to creating recursive, self-improving loops. Organizations must treat their internal processes as software that continuously optimizes itself through agentic feedback.

## Key points
- Shift from static workflows to dynamic, self-correcting agentic loops.
- Leverage AI to iterate on the company's own operational logic.
- Focus on recursive improvement cycles where output quality informs process refinement.
- Integrate AI-native principles at the foundational level rather than as an overlay.

## Why this matters for Jake
This framework provides a blueprint for advising clients on institutionalizing AI, moving them from 'AI-enabled' to 'AI-native' to create a sustainable competitive advantage.
