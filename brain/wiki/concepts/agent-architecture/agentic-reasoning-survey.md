# Agentic Reasoning — The Three-Layer Framework
**Source**: [[2026-05-11-linkedin-articles-inspiration]] (Wei et al., arXiv:2601.12538)
**Last updated**: 2026-05-11

## Core insight

"Agentic Reasoning" is the paradigm shift where LLMs stop being answer machines and become autonomous agents — bridging **thought and action** across long-horizon, multi-step tasks.

## The three-layer stack

| Layer | What it covers |
|-------|---------------|
| **Foundational** | Planning, tool use, search in stable environments — the basics of acting in the world |
| **Self-Evolving** | Agents that refine their own skills via feedback loops, memory, and adaptation |
| **Collective** | Multi-agent coordination, shared goals, emergent behavior at scale |

## Why this matters

Most current "agent" frameworks only operate at Layer 1. Layers 2–3 are where compounding intelligence lives — and where competitive moats get built.

The paper argues that reliable agents require world modeling + long-horizon interaction, not just better prompts. This aligns directly with [[runbooks-and-production-agent-ops]]: scaffolding over prompting.
