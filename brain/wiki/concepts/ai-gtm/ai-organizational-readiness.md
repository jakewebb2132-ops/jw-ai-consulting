# AI Organizational Readiness
**Source**: [[2026-05-06-linkedin-articles-inspiration]] (Daniel Miessler, "Most Companies Aren't Anywhere Near Ready for AI")
**Last updated**: 2026-05-06

## Core insight

AI is an execution engine. It is powerless without clear instructions. The bottleneck is not AI capability — it's company self-awareness. Companies that thrive with AI are the same companies that already knew exactly what they were doing before AI existed.

## The chaotic black box problem

Most companies are "haphazardly successful despite themselves." They can't articulate:
- What problem they solve for customers
- What their goals and metrics are
- What challenges block those goals
- What strategies address those challenges
- What projects implement those strategies
- What it costs

These companies will get nothing from AI except more impressive flailing.

## The competitive threat framing

- Small, organized companies can now punch like large enterprises
- Large, disorganized companies face "extraordinary downward force"
- AI is not the differentiator in phase 1 — operational clarity is
- AI becomes the weapon winners use to fight each other in phase 2

## The diagnostic checklist (for prospect conversations)

A company is AI-ready if it can answer quickly and consistently across quarters:
1. What problem do you solve?
2. What's wrong with existing solutions?
3. What are your company goals?
4. What are the metrics on those goals?
5. What challenges block you?
6. What strategies address those challenges?
7. What projects implement those strategies?
8. Who does what, and what does it cost?
