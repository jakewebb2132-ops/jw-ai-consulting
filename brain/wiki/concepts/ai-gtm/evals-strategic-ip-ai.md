# Evals as the Strategic IP for AI
**Source**: [[2026-06-24-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/garrettlord/status/2068754262440767500?s=46&t=hKsCOqDSqhQTmxGk_H5iPQ)
**Last updated**: 2026-06-24

## Core insight
Companies are shifting from 'AI experimentation' to 'AI ROI' by realizing that proprietary evaluation frameworks are the true competitive moat. If you cannot measure performance against your specific business context, you cannot scale or trust the output.

## Key points
* Executives are moving past the 'AI hype' phase into a rigorous demand for measurable ROI.
* Standard benchmarks are insufficient; proprietary evals are the new strategic IP.
* Evals act as the feedback loop that allows for iterative improvement of agentic workflows.
* Organizations that own their evaluation data will outpace those relying on generic model performance.

## Why this matters for Jake
This validates the shift toward 'AI Organizational Readiness' where my consulting focus moves from simple implementation to building the internal evaluation infrastructure that sustains long-term value.
