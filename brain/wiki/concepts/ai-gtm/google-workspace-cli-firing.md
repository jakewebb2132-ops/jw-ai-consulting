# Corporate Friction and the Google Workspace CLI
**Source**: [[2026-06-24-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/steipete/status/2069594195522941059?s=46&t=hKsCOqDSqhQTmxGk_H5iPQ)
**Last updated**: 2026-06-24

## Core insight
Large organizations often suffer from an institutional knowledge tax where internal innovation is punished rather than rewarded. When high-utility tools like the Google Workspace CLI emerge, they expose the gap between employee-driven productivity and rigid corporate compliance structures.

## Key points
* Justin Poehnelt was terminated by Google for creating a viral, high-utility CLI tool for Workspace.
* The tool achieved massive organic adoption, hitting #1 on Hacker News and gaining thousands of GitHub stars.
* Corporate environments often view unauthorized productivity tools as security or brand liabilities rather than assets.
* The incident highlights the fragility of internal "intrapreneurship" within massive, legacy-structured tech companies.

## Why this matters for Jake
This serves as a cautionary tale for GTM strategies involving enterprise adoption; it highlights the friction between bottom-up developer advocacy and top-down institutional control.
