# The Evolution of Engineering Productivity with AI
**Source**: [[2026-07-12-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/neilrahilly/status/2075290325757608148?s=20)
**Last updated**: 2026-07-12

## Core insight
The traditional 1968 paradigm of the '10x engineer' is being fundamentally disrupted by AI-pilling. We are moving from a world where individual output is capped by human cognition to one where organizational leverage is defined by how effectively teams integrate AI agents into their core workflows.

## Key points
* AI-pilling shifts the focus from hiring individual superstars to building systems that amplify average engineer output.
* The 1968 study on software productivity is no longer the ceiling; AI tools redefine the baseline for developer velocity.
* Organizational success now depends on the speed of internal adoption and the removal of friction in AI-assisted development cycles.
* Companies that fail to integrate AI into their engineering culture will face a widening productivity gap against AI-native competitors.

## Why this matters for Jake
This validates the shift toward AI-organizational readiness, helping clients move beyond tool implementation to true operational transformation. It reinforces the need for GTM strategies that prioritize AI-driven efficiency as a primary competitive moat.
