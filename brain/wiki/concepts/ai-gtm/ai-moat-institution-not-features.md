# The Real AI Moat: Institution, Not Features
**Source**: [[2026-05-11-linkedin-articles-inspiration]] (Jaya Gupta @JayaGup10)
**Last updated**: 2026-05-11

## Core insight

As AI makes product features trivially easy to imitate, the real competitive moat is the **institution underneath** — the way a company organizes ambition, concentrates judgment, and turns work into a compounding system.

## The feature parity problem

AI collapses the time-to-feature from months to days. Every feature advantage evaporates. What can't be copied:
- How the company **makes decisions**
- How the company **learns from mistakes**
- How the company **concentrates expertise** into repeatable systems
- The **culture of compounding** — building on what you built

## What "institution" actually means in practice

- Decision-making frameworks that outlast individual employees
- Knowledge capture systems that make every conversation searchable
- Judgment codified into processes (not just people)
- Feedback loops that make the system smarter over time
