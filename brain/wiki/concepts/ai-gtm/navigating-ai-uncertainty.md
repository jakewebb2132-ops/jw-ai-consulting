# Navigating AI Uncertainty Across Every Layer
**Source**: [[2026-05-22-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/thisisgrantlee/status/2054192474671943855?s=46&t=hKsCOqDSqhQTmxGk_H5iPQ)
**Last updated**: 2026-05-22

## Core insight
AI-driven disruption isn't just a bottom-up threat; it is creating a state of diffuse anxiety across every layer of the professional stack. To thrive, you must move beyond reactive safety-seeking and build conviction in work that provides genuine, non-replicable purpose.

## Key points
- Foundation models are in a perpetual, high-speed race, forcing constant adaptation even at the top.
- App-layer companies must build deep network effects and real value, as simple prompt-based features are easily commoditized.
- Incumbents are currently stalling by bolting AI onto legacy products rather than fundamentally transforming their operations.
- Knowledge workers are shifting from passive observers to active experimenters to maintain relevance.
- New graduates face a redefined entry-level landscape where traditional career on-ramps are becoming increasingly narrow and unpredictable.

## Why this matters for Jake
This framework helps clients understand that organizational readiness is a psychological challenge as much as a technical one, allowing for better GTM positioning during periods of market instability.
