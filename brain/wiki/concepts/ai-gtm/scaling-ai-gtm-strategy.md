# Scaling AI Go-To-Market Strategy Effectively
**Source**: [[2026-10-11-111019-linkedin-ingest]] (https://x.com/N01ennn/status/2108182955671580771?s=20)
**Last updated**: 2026-10-11

## Core insight
Successfully taking AI products to market requires bridging the gap between raw technical capability and clear business value. Positioning must focus on measurable operational outcomes rather than underlying model features.

## Key points
- Align product messaging directly with buyer pain points and existing workflows.
- Avoid overly technical jargon that obscures the core business value proposition.
- Build repeatable GTM motions that scale alongside product iterations.
- Leverage concrete case studies to prove reliability and ROI.

## Why this matters for Jake
This framework informs how I advise AI startups on positioning their solutions for maximum market traction and enterprise adoption.
